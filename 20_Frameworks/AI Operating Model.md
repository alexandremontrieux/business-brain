# AI Operating Model

> AE target operating model for AI — "Aligning people, process & technology every step in the journey." Organise yourself to combine *doing the right things* with *doing things right*.

![[ai-operating-model.png]]

## What it is

A target operating model (TOM) for scaling AI in an organisation. It frames AI adoption across four pillars — **Business, Process, People, Technology** — wrapped by a continuous loop that runs from **Guiding AI Strategy** through delivery and back via **Feedback Operations**. The outer loop is governed by four verbs: *Provide* value & principles, *Communicate* vision & perspective, *Evaluate* value & realisation, and *Evaluate* support & engagement.

## When to use it

- Assessing an organisation's AI maturity and spotting gaps across the four pillars.
- Structuring an AI adoption roadmap or transformation programme.
- Aligning stakeholders on how strategy, governance, people, and platforms fit together.
- As a client-facing reference model in AI strategy / advisory engagements.

## Structure / steps

**The central journey (flywheel: Guiding AI Strategy → Feedback Operations)**

1. **Use cases** — *business value definition.* Underpinned by **Awareness**: level the knowledge gap.
2. **Experimentation** — *learning by trying things out.* Underpinned by **Pilots**: test & validate feasibility & value.
3. **Embedding** — *incorporate the solution into processes.* Underpinned by **Ambassadors**: share & showcase successes.
4. **Community** — *innovation as part of the group.* Underpinned by **Monitor, Maintain & Improve**: continuous improvement.

**The four pillars**

- **Business** — the strategy foundation. Value definition and executive sponsorship feed a strategy stack: Business Strategy (mission & vision) → Data & AI Strategy, Digital Strategy, and other [XYZ] strategies.
- **Process** — AI Leadership (Architecture Board ↔ Portfolio Management, over Project & Program Management). Governed by **Governance**: Roles & Responsibilities, Policies & Procedures, Mandate & Scope, KPIs & Value; plus Data Governance and other domain governance. Responsible-AI principles run alongside: Transparency, Ethics, Diversity, Compliance, Reliability, Risk, Bias, Trust. Delivery concerns: Stakeholders, Documentation, Prioritisation, Reusability, Quality.
- **People** — Culture (Engagement, Ownership, Involvement, Trial & Error, Empowerment, Tools & Support; plus Collaboration, Leadership, Communication, Trust, Creativity, Values, Inspiration, Performance). Driven by an **AI Enablement Cell** covering Change Management and Learning & Development, and enablement offers: Training, Literacy, Playground, Reusability, Coaching, Talent Leads.
- **Technology** — two platforms on shared IT & digital infrastructure (Buy vs Build, Cloud vs On-premise, Platform Engineering, Monitoring, SDLC):
  - **Data Platform** — Data Security, AI/ML(Ops) Mgt, Master Data Mgt, Storage & Operations, Compliance; Operational Data, Reporting, Lineage, Catalogue, Data Quality, Data Model → Data Products.
  - **AI Platform** — Machine Learning & Generative AI; Training, Inference, Feature Store, LLM, Human-in-the-loop, RAG, Agents; Guardrails → AI Product.

## Inputs & outputs

- **Inputs:** business strategy & sponsorship, a governance mandate, data and AI platform capabilities, and a culture/enablement engine.
- **Outputs:** validated AI use cases moved from pilot to embedded process, Data Products and AI Products, and a feedback loop that re-informs strategy.
